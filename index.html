Selected research

Learning to Coach for Experiential Learning

Guanheng Chen*, Tianzhu Ye*, Li Dong*, Xun Wu, Shaohan Huang, Furu Wei

Train a coach to turn an actor’s past attempts into useful guidance. The actor stays frozen; the coach learns from its downstream performance, enabling iterative improvement and transferable experiential knowledge.

Paper ↗PDF ↗

LLM-as-a-Coach: Experiential Learning for Non-Verifiable Tasks

Tianzhu Ye*, Li Dong*, Guanheng Chen, He Zhu, Xun Wu, Shaohan Huang, Furu Wei

Learn from rich natural-language feedback on open-ended tasks. Experiential knowledge is internalized through on-policy context distillation, providing a richer learning signal than scalar rewards.
